## Army rule

### Reanimation Protocols
At the end of your Command phase, every Necrons unit on the table heals **D3 wounds**. Healing can bring back destroyed models: a 1-wound model returns for each wound healed once the live models are topped up. Warriors re-roll the D3, a Reanimator within 3" adds another D3, and the *Undying Legions* stratagem triggers it out of sequence.

## Detachment: Awakened Dynasty (3 DP)
- **Command Protocols:** a unit led by a Necrons Character gets **+1 to hit** on every attack. This is the whole point of the detachment, so attach characters to the units you expect to fight with.
- **Enhancements (any Necrons character unless noted):**
  - *Veil of Darkness* (20): once per battle at the end of the enemy's turn, if the bearer's unit isn't engaged, lift it into strategic reserves with Deep Strike. It must come back next turn.
  - *Nether-realm Casket* (20): the bearer's unit has Stealth.
  - *Phasal Subjugator* (35): aura, friendly non-character Necrons units within 6" get +1 to hit.
  - *Enaegic Dermal Bond* (30): the bearer has a 4+ Feel No Pain.
- **Stratagems:**
  - *Eternal Revenant* (1CP): bring back a dead Necrons infantry character near where it died, with half its wounds. Once per model per battle.
  - *Undying Legions* (1CP): after an enemy unit shoots or fights, a Necrons unit that lost models reanimates immediately: D3 wounds, D3+1 if a character leads it.
  - *Hungry Void* (1CP, Fight phase): the unit's melee weapons get +1 S; +1 AP as well if a character leads it.
  - *Sudden Storm* (1CP, Movement phase): the unit's guns get Assault; re-roll Advance rolls as well if a character leads it.
  - *Conquering Tyrant* (1CP, Shooting phase): re-roll hit rolls of 1 at half range; full hit re-rolls if a character leads it.
  - *Vengeful Stars* (2CP): after an enemy unit destroys a Necrons unit, a Necrons character nearby shoots at the attacker.

## Attachments

What's set in the export, and why:

- **Orikan supports the 10 Warriors.** The 4+ invulnerable save turns a 4+ armour unit into something that holds a point. Szeras isn't a leader, so stand him within 3" for the AP aura instead.
- **Technomancer supports a Wraith unit.** The only multi-wound unit he can join, so his D3 heal revives Wraiths. 5+ Feel No Pain on top of their 4+ invulnerable save.
- **Still unattached:** Royal Warden and Trazyn (Immortals or Warriors; Trazyn also Lychguard), Skorpekh Lord (Skorpekh Destroyers). Each bodyguard unit can take one Leader plus one Support. The Warriors already have their Support slot used by Orikan, so a Warden or Trazyn can still lead them.
- **Cryptothralls** can join a unit that Orikan or the Technomancer is attached to (both are Crypteks). That gives the Cryptek a 4+ Feel No Pain and adds two 3-wound bodies.

## Units

Statline format: **M / T / Sv / W / Ld / OC / invulnerable save**. Weapon format: range · attacks · to-hit · S · AP · D · keywords. Points are per unit as taken in the list.

### C'tan Shard of the Void Dragon (345; Epic Hero, Deep Strike)
- **Stats:** 10" / 11 / 3+ / 16 / 6+ / 4 / 4+. 5+ Feel No Pain. Deadly Demise D6. Can't be Warlord.
- **Necrodermis:** every attack allocated to him does 1 less damage.
- **Matter Absorption:** start of your Shooting phase, pick an enemy vehicle within 12"; on a 2+ it takes D3 mortal wounds and he heals that many.
- **Spear (shooting):** 12" · D3 · 2+ · 8 · -3 · D6+2 · Anti-Vehicle 2+.
- **Voltaic storm:** 18" · D6+3 · 2+ · 7 · -1 · 2 · Blast, Sustained Hits 2.
- **Melee:** spear strike 5 · 2+ · 12 · -4 · D6+2 (Anti-Vehicle 2+), or sweep 10 · 2+ · 8 · -1 · 2. Plus tail blades 6 · 2+ · 6 · -1 · 1 (Extra Attacks).

### Illuminor Szeras (175; Epic Hero, Warlord)
- **Stats:** 8" / 8 / 2+ / 9 / 6+ / 3 / 4+. 4+ Feel No Pain.
- **Lone Operative** while within 3" of another Necrons unit.
- **Mechanical Augmentation (aura):** Battleline units within 3" get +1 AP on their attacks, and attacks against them get -1 AP. Each Fight phase he kills something in, the aura grows 3", up to 12".
- **Eldritch lance:** 36" · 3 · 3+ · 9 · -3 · 3. Melee 4 · 3+ · 9 · -3 · 3, plus impaling legs 4 · 3+ · 6 · -1 · 1 (Extra Attacks).
- **Not a leader.** He walks next to the Warriors or Immortals.

### Orikan the Diviner (90; Support for Immortals or Warriors)
- **Stats:** 5" / 4 / 4+ / 4 / 6+ / 1 / 4+.
- **Master Chronomancer:** his unit has a **4+ invulnerable save**.
- **The Stars Are Right:** once per battle at the start of the Fight phase, his staff triples to 6 attacks at S12, and every wound is a critical wound (so every wound becomes D3 mortal wounds through Devastating Wounds).
- **Staff of Tomorrow:** 2 · 3+ · 4 · -3 · D3 · Devastating Wounds.

### Trazyn the Infinite (60; Leader for Immortals, Lychguard or Warriors)
- **Stats:** 5" / 5 / 2+ / 6 / 6+ / 1 / 4+.
- **Ancient Collector:** at the end of your Command phase, an objective his unit is on stays yours after they leave, until the enemy takes it.
- **Surrogate Hosts:** start of your Command phase, pick another Necrons infantry character (not the Skorpekh Lord or an Epic Hero), remove it, and put Trazyn in its place with his current wounds. He takes over leading that unit.
- **Empathic Obliterator:** 4 · 2+ · 7 · 0 · D3 · Sustained Hits D3.

### Hexmark Destroyer (75; Deep Strike, Lone Operative)
- **Stats:** 8" / 5 / 3+ / 5 / 6+ / 1 / –.
- **Inescapable Death:** once per turn, Fire Overwatch with him for 0CP even if you've already used it, and he hits on 2+ when he does.
- **Multi-threat Eliminator:** once per turn in the enemy Shooting phase, when a Necrons unit within 3" of him is shot, he shoots back at the attacker.
- **Pistols:** 18" · 6 · 2+ · 6 · -2 · 1 · Ignores Cover, Pistol. Melee 4 · 3+ · 5 · 0 · 1.

### Royal Warden (50; Leader for Immortals or Warriors)
- **Stats:** 5" / 5 / 3+ / 4 / 6+ / 1 / –.
- **Adaptive Strategy:** his unit can shoot and charge after falling back.
- **Once per battle:** remove battle-shock from a Necrons unit within 12".
- **Relic gauss blaster:** 24" · 2 · 3+ · 5 · -1 · 2 · Lethal Hits, Rapid Fire 2.

### Skorpekh Lord (95; Leader for Skorpekh Destroyers)
- **Stats:** 8" / 7 / 3+ / 7 / 6+ / 2 / 4+.
- **United in Destruction:** his unit's melee weapons get Lethal Hits.
- **Crimson Harvest:** after a charge move, one enemy unit in engagement range takes D3 mortal wounds on a 2-5, D3+3 on a 6.
- **Weapons:** enmitic annihilator 18" · 2 · 2+ · 6 · -1 · 1 (Rapid Fire 2); hyperphase harvester 4 · 2+ · 10 · -3 · 3; flensing claw 8 · 2+ · 6 · -1 · 1.

### Technomancer (80; Support for Wraiths, Immortals or Warriors)
- **Stats:** 10" / 4 / 4+ / 4 / 6+ / 1 / –.
- **Rites of Reanimation:** his unit has a **5+ Feel No Pain**.
- **Technomancer:** end of your Movement phase, one Necrons model within 6" heals up to D3 wounds.
- **Staff of light:** 18" · 3 · 4+ · 5 · -2 · 1; melee 2 · 4+ · 5 · -2 · 1.
