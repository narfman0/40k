## Army rule

### Martial Ka'tah
Every Custodes unit (not the Sisters of Silence) picks a stance each time it's selected to fight, for that fight only:
- **Dacatarai:** melee weapons get **Sustained Hits 1**.
- **Rendax:** melee weapons get **Lethal Hits**.

Rule of thumb: Rendax into high-toughness targets where wounding is the problem, Dacatarai into infantry where you want more hits. The Shield-Captain can run both at once, once per battle.

## Detachment: Shield Host (2 DP)
- **Martial Mastery:** at the start of each battle round pick one, for the round:
  - melee attacks from Ka'tah units score **critical hits on 5+**, or
  - Ka'tah units' melee weapons get **+1 AP**.
  Critical hits on 5+ doubles the payoff from either stance, so that's the default; take +1 AP against 2+ saves.
- **Enhancements:**
  - *Auric Mantle* (15, Shield-Captain or Blade Champion): +2 wounds. **Taken, on the Blade Champion.**
  - *Castellan's Mark* (20, Shield-Captain): after deployment, redeploy up to two Custodes units, including into strategic reserves beyond the normal limit.
  - *From the Hall of Armouries* (20, Shield-Captain): +1 S and +1 D on the bearer's melee weapons.
  - *Panoptispex* (5, Shield-Captain or Blade Champion): the bearer's unit's guns ignore cover.
- **Stratagems (1CP each):**
  - *Arcane Genetic Alchemy:* when a unit takes mortal wounds, 4+ Feel No Pain against mortal wounds for the phase.
  - *Avenge the Fallen* (start of Fight phase): a unit that has lost models gets +1 attack on melee weapons, +2 if below half strength.
  - *Unwavering Sentinels* (Fight phase): Custodes infantry on an objective you control are hit at -1 in melee for the phase.
  - *Multipotentiality* (Movement phase): a unit that fell back can still shoot and charge.
  - *Vigilance Eternal* (Movement phase): an objective a Custodian Guard unit is on stays yours after they leave.
  - *Archeotech Munitions* (Shooting phase): a unit's guns get Lethal Hits or Sustained Hits 1 before it shoots.

## Attachments

Set in the export:

- **Trajann leads one Custodian Guard squad.** They ignore hit modifiers, and he's the Warlord.
- **Blade Champion leads the other Custodian Guard squad.** Re-rolled charges, and one advance-and-charge per battle.
- **Shield-Captain is unattached.** He can only lead Guard, Wardens or Sagittarum, and both Guard squads are taken. Either swap him for the Blade Champion on one squad or accept he runs solo; 170 pts are still spare in the list.
- Allarus, Praetors and Prosecutors have no leaders in this list.

## Units

Statline format: **M / T / Sv / W / Ld / OC / invulnerable save**. Weapon format: range · attacks · to-hit · S · AP · D · keywords. Points are per unit as taken in the list. Every Custodes unit except the Prosecutors has Martial Ka'tah; the infantry and characters all have Deep Strike.

### Trajann Valoris (135; Epic Hero, Warlord)
- **Stats:** 6" / 6 / 2+ / 7 / 5+ / 3 / 4+. 5+ Feel No Pain.
- **Captain-General:** his unit ignores modifiers to hit rolls, BS and WS.
- **Moment Shackle:** once per battle at the start of the Fight phase, either his axe has 12 attacks or he has a 2+ invulnerable save, for the phase.
- **Watcher's Axe:** 6 · 2+ · 10 · -2 · 3. **Eagle's Scream:** 24" · 2 · 2+ · 5 · -2 · 3 · Assault.
- **Leads:** Custodian Guard (either kind), Wardens, Sagittarum.

### Blade Champion (110 + 15 Auric Mantle)
- **Stats:** 6" / 6 / 2+ / 8 with the Mantle / 6+ / 3 / 4+.
- **Swift Onslaught:** re-roll charge rolls for his unit.
- **Martial Inspiration:** once per battle, his unit can charge after advancing.
- **Vaultswords, pick one profile each fight:** Behemor 6 · 2+ · 7 · -2 · 2 (Precision); Hurricanis 9 · 2+ · 5 · -1 · 1 (Sustained Hits 1); Victus 5 · 2+ · 6 · -3 · 3 (Devastating Wounds).
- **Leads:** the same units as Trajann.

### Shield-Captain (110)
- **Stats:** 6" / 6 / 2+ / 6 / 6+ / 2 / 4+.
- **Master of the Stances:** once per battle, both Ka'tah stances at once for one fight.
- **Strategic Mastery:** once per battle round, a stratagem used on his unit costs 1CP less.
- **Guardian spear:** melee 7 · 2+ · 7 · -2 · 2; shooting 24" · 2 · 2+ · 5 · -1 · 2 · Assault.
- **Leads:** the same units as Trajann.

### Custodian Guard (225 for 5; Battleline; two squads)
- **Stats:** 6" / 6 / 2+ / 3 / 6+ / 3 / 4+. The vexilla model has 4 wounds from its shield.
- **Stand Vigil:** re-roll wound rolls of 1; full wound re-rolls while on an objective you control.
- **Sentinel Storm:** once per battle, shoot twice in one Shooting phase.
- **Vexilla:** +1 OC for every model in the unit (so 4 each).
- **Guardian spear:** melee 5 · 2+ · 7 · -2 · 2; shooting 24" · 2 · 2+ · 5 · -1 · 2 · Assault. The vexilla model fights with a misericordia 5 · 2+ · 5 · -2 · 1.

### Allarus Custodians (120 for 2; two squads)
- **Stats:** 5" / 7 / 2+ / 4 / 6+ / 2 / 4+.
- **Slayers of Tyrants:** re-roll wound rolls against Characters, Monsters and Vehicles.
- **From Golden Light:** once per battle at the end of the enemy's turn, if not engaged, go back into strategic reserves and Deep Strike again later.
- **Weapons:** guardian spear as above; balistus grenade launcher 18" · D6 · 2+ · 4 · -1 · 1 · Blast.

### Prosecutors (55 for 5; Sisters of Silence; two squads)
- **Stats:** 6" / 3 / 3+ / 1 / 6+ / 2 / –. No Ka'tah.
- **Daughters of the Abyss:** 3+ Feel No Pain against psychic attacks and mortal wounds.
- **Purity of Execution:** shooting at Psykers gets Precision and Devastating Wounds.
- **Boltgun:** 24" · 1 · 3+ · 5 · -1 · 1 · Rapid Fire 1. Melee 2 · 3+ · 3 · 0 · 1.
- Cheap objective sitters and action-doers. Keep them out of fights.

### Vertus Praetors (230 for 3)
- **Stats:** 12" / 7 / 2+ / 5 / 6+ / 2 / 4+.
- **Turbo Boost:** advancing is a flat +6" instead of a roll.
- **Quicksilver Execution:** once per battle after a move, pick a non-monster, non-vehicle enemy unit they moved over and roll a D6 per Praetor; each 2+ is 2 mortal wounds.
- **Weapons:** interceptor lance 5 · 2+ · 7 · -2 · 2 (Lance: +1 to wound on the charge); salvo launcher 24" · 1 · 2+ · 10 · -3 · D6+1 · Twin-linked.

### Caladius Grav-tank (210)
- **Stats:** 10" / 11 / 2+ / 14 / 6+ / 4 / 5+. Deadly Demise D3. -1 to hit at 5 wounds or fewer.
- **Advanced Firepower:** the accelerator cannon gets Lethal Hits against anything that isn't a monster or vehicle.
- **Twin iliastus accelerator cannon:** 48" · 4 · 2+ · 10 · -1 · 3 · Rapid Fire 4, Twin-linked. **Twin lastrum bolt cannon:** 36" · 3 · 2+ · 6 · -1 · 1 · Sustained Hits 1. Hull 4 · 4+ · 6 · 0 · 1.

### Venerable Land Raider (220; Transport)
- **Stats:** 10" / 12 / 2+ / 16 / 6+ / 5 / –. Deadly Demise D6. -1 to hit at 5 wounds or fewer.
- **Carries** 6 Custodes infantry models. Terminators (Allarus) usually take two spaces each; check the datasheet.
- **Assault Ramp:** a unit that gets out after the tank's normal move can still charge.
- **Weapons:** 2 Godhammer lascannons, each 48" · 2 · 2+ · 12 · -3 · D6+1; twin heavy bolter 36" · 3 · 2+ · 5 · -1 · 2 · Sustained Hits 1, Twin-linked; tracks 6 · 4+ · 8 · 0 · 1.
