## Paint

- **Scheme:** Mars red.
- **Done:** about half the army is painted well in red. Cawl and one Onager are the reference models to match.
- **Orange models:** some are painted orange and need repainting red. Paint over them; don't strip.
- **Unpainted:** everything else.
- **Paints owned:** see [Paints](../paints.md).

### Working recipe

This is a suggestion built from the paints I own. Compare it against Cawl before committing to it, and record any changes here.

- **Primer:** black spray, or Wraithbone if it's the spray. Paint Mephiston Red straight over the old orange; the orange underneath only warms it.
- **Robes and armour plates:** Mephiston Red, two thin coats. Shade the recesses only with Army Painter Red Tone. It deepens the red without browning it. Strong Tone also works for a dirtier look. Tidy up with Mephiston Red. Edge highlight with Evil Sunz Scarlet; add Wild Rider Red later for a finer top edge.
- **Metal (bionics, guns, hulls):** Leadbelcher, then Strong Tone for a warm, grimy steel, or Nuln Oil for a cleaner, cooler steel.
- **Trim and cogs:** Retributor Armour, Nuln Oil in the recesses, then a Liberator Gold edge highlight.
- **Cabling, leather, wood:** Rhinox Hide, then Strong Tone.
- **Bone, parchment, purity seals:** Zandri Dust, washed with Strong Tone, then highlighted with Wraithbone or Corax White.
- **Lenses and power glow:** Baharroth Blue, with a Corax White dot.
- **Black parts (cloth, suits, off items):** Corvus Black for small detail, or Vallejo Black or Army Painter Matt Black for big flat areas. Edge the folds with Corvus Black mixed with a little Corax White.
- **Bases:** match Cawl and the Onager if they already have a base style. Otherwise, apply Martian Ironearth thickly and let it dry fully (it cracks as it dries). Wash with Strong Tone, drybrush Zandri Dust, and paint the rim Rhinox Hide or Corvus Black.

### Battle-ready plan

Goal: every model in the list primed, with at least three colours and a finished base. That's Games Workshop's "battle ready" standard and what most tournaments ask for. Highlights and gold trim can come later.

**Fast recipe** (batches of 5–10 models):
1. Prime black, or Wraithbone if it's the spray.
2. Mephiston Red on robes and armour plates. Old orange models need two coats.
3. Leadbelcher on metal.
4. One detail colour: Rhinox Hide on cabling, or Zandri Dust on bone.
5. Wash: Red Tone on the red, Strong Tone on everything else (metal, cabling, bone). If you're short on time, Strong Tone over the whole model is fine.
6. Mephiston Red again on the raised areas of the red. Keep this step, so the batch doesn't look darker than the models that are already done.
7. A Baharroth Blue dot on lenses.
8. Base: Martian Ironearth, Strong Tone, a Zandri Dust drybrush, and a black or brown rim.

**Upgrade to match the finished models later:** Evil Sunz Scarlet edge highlight, Retributor Armour trim, Nuln Oil in the metal recesses.

**Order:**
1. Bases on every model, including the finished ones, so the army matches.
2. Infantry blocks: Rangers and Vanguard, ten at a time.
3. The new Combat Patrol models, as they're built.
4. Characters and vehicles.

**Reference look (Cawl):** matte, lighter and dusty red, not deep and saturated. To match it:
- **Wash:** Strong Tone in the recesses only. Skip Red Tone on anything that should match Cawl, because it deepens the red further.
- **Lighten:** mix Mephiston Red with a little Zandri Dust (about 3:1) and drybrush or sponge it over the raised areas of the panels. Evil Sunz Scarlet gives a brighter, less dusty lift.
- **Dust:** lightly drybrush Zandri Dust on the legs and feet, fading out going up.
- **Finish:** matte varnish to remove wash sheen.
- **Test first:** try it on the back of one Kastelan and compare with Cawl before doing the rest.

**Kastelan tips:** the faceplate is a glass visor, not metal. Paint it as glass over the existing black: Baharroth Blue in a curve along the lower half, a thinner, brighter band of Baharroth mixed with Corax White near the bottom edge, then a small Corax White glint in the top corner. Keep the wash off the visor. Paint the visor, metals and gold before washing. On the big flat panels, put the wash only into the recesses and panel lines; an all-over wash leaves tide marks.
